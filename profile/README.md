# OMO AI

**Georgian voice AI that turns business calls into measurable actions.**

OMO AI helps businesses answer and automate customer calls in Georgian, 24/7. The platform connects conversations with real business data and workflows so calls can become confirmed bookings, qualified sales leads, structured feedback, or correctly routed actions.

## What OMO supports

- AI-powered inbound call handling
- Scheduled outbound sales campaigns
- Appointment and meeting booking
- Customer satisfaction and NPS surveys
- Intelligent voice menus and call routing
- Calendar, CRM, API and webhook integrations
- Human handoff for complex or sensitive cases
- Audio, transcript, event and outcome audit trails

## Built for reliable business operations

OMO verifies information before taking critical actions, asks clarifying questions when responses are uncertain, and keeps every session measurable and reviewable.
