# OMO AI

**Georgian voice AI for business calls and verified workflows.**

OMO AI is a Georgian-language voice platform for business phone workflows. It connects conversations with business data so a call can lead to an appointment, a qualified sales lead, structured feedback or the right routing decision.

## What OMO supports

- Inbound call handling and scheduled outbound campaigns
- Appointment and meeting booking
- Customer feedback and NPS/CSAT surveys
- Voice menus and call routing
- Calendar, CRM, API and webhook integrations
- Clarification and human handoff
- Call transcripts, events and outcome review

Available integrations, working hours, recording and operational limits depend on the configured deployment. OMO does not promise error-free recognition, unrestricted autonomy or guaranteed completion of every call.

## A conversation is not proof of a completed action

OMO's documented approach is to check relevant business data, confirm critical details when required and verify the result in the destination system. Uncertain responses require clarification; an uncertain write outcome requires reconciliation rather than a blind retry.
